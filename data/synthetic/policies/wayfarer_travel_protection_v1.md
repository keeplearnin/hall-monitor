# Wayfarer Travel Protection — Policy Wording (v1)

Issued by Lantern Ridge Mutual, a fictional insurer. This wording is synthetic and written for testing
only. It is not based on any real insurer's policy and must not be used as insurance advice.

## Schedule of benefits

All amounts are in US dollars, per insured person, per trip.

| Benefit | Essentials | Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Trip cancellation (section 2) | 3,000 | 7,500 |
| Baggage delay, per full or part day (section 3.1) | 100 | 150 |
| Baggage delay, maximum days | 3 | 5 |
| Baggage loss (section 3.2) | 1,000 | 2,500 |
| Baggage loss, any one item | 500 | 500 |
| Baggage loss, all valuables combined | 750 | 1,000 |
| Emergency medical expenses (section 4) | 50,000 | 250,000 |
| Emergency medical deductible | 100 | 0 |
| Pre-existing condition waiver (section 5.1) | Not available | Available |

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Insured trip.** The trip named on the policy record, from the departure date to the return date.

1.2 **Pre-existing condition.** An illness, injury or medical condition for which, in the 90 days
before the policy purchase date, the insured person received medical advice, diagnosis, care or
treatment, or had a change in prescribed medication.

1.3 **Family member.** Spouse or domestic partner, child, parent, sibling or grandparent.

1.4 **Common carrier.** An airline, train, bus or ship operator licensed to carry paying passengers.

1.5 **Valuables.** Jewellery, watches, cameras, laptops, tablets, phones and other portable
electronics.

1.6 **Reasonable and customary charges.** Charges in line with what other providers in the same area
charge for similar treatment.

## 2. Trip cancellation

2.1 **Cover.** We reimburse prepaid, non-refundable trip costs if the insured person cancels the
insured trip before departure because of:
(a) sudden illness or injury of the insured person or a family member that a doctor certifies makes
travel inadvisable;
(b) death of the insured person or a family member;
(c) a strike or natural disaster that stops the common carrier operating for at least 24 hours.

2.2 **Limit.** Up to the trip cancellation amount in the schedule of benefits.

2.3 **Prompt cancellation.** The insured person must cancel with the travel suppliers within 72 hours
of learning of the reason for cancellation. If they do not, we pay only the cancellation charges
that would have applied at the end of those 72 hours.

2.4 **Waiting period.** We do not cover cancellation for a reason that first arises within 14 days of
the policy purchase date, unless the policy was bought within 14 days of the initial trip deposit.

## 3. Baggage

3.1 **Baggage delay.** If a common carrier delays the insured person's checked baggage by more than
12 hours on the outbound journey, we reimburse reasonable purchases of essential toiletries and
clothing, up to the daily amount for each full or part 24-hour period of delay, up to the maximum
days in the schedule. Itemised receipts are required.

3.2 **Baggage loss.** We pay the actual cash value of checked baggage and personal effects that are
permanently lost, stolen or damaged during the insured trip, up to the baggage loss amount, the
per-item limit and the combined valuables limit in the schedule. Proof of ownership and value is
required for any item over 100.

3.3 **Carrier report.** Loss of or delay to checked baggage must be reported to the common carrier
and a written carrier report (such as a property irregularity report) obtained before leaving the
airport or terminal. Without it, we may reduce or decline the claim unless the insured person shows
it was not reasonably possible to obtain one.

## 4. Emergency medical expenses

4.1 **Cover.** We pay reasonable and customary charges for emergency treatment of a sudden illness or
injury that first occurs during the insured trip, up to the emergency medical amount in the schedule,
less the deductible.

4.2 **Notification.** For treatment expected to cost more than 10,000, the insured person or someone
on their behalf must notify our assistance service within 48 hours.

## 5. General exclusions

We do not pay any claim arising from:

5.1 **Pre-existing conditions** of the insured person, a travelling companion or a family member. On
the Plus plan this exclusion is waived if the policy was bought within 14 days of the initial trip
deposit and the insured person was fit to travel on the purchase date.

5.2 Elective or non-emergency treatment, or travel undertaken to obtain medical treatment.

5.3 Travel against the advice of a doctor.

5.4 The insured person being under the influence of alcohol or drugs, unless prescribed by a doctor.

5.5 Hazardous activities: mountaineering or climbing that requires ropes or guides, skydiving,
paragliding, motor racing and professional sport. Hiking on marked trails is not a hazardous
activity.

5.6 **Fraud.** If any claim is fraudulent or deliberately exaggerated, we will not pay it and may
cancel the policy.

## 6. Making a claim

6.1 Claims must be submitted within 90 days of the incident.

6.2 Original itemised receipts, invoices and reports must be provided. Each receipt may be claimed
once.

6.3 We may ask for more information, a medical report or a statement before deciding a claim.
