### claims_supervisor

- **Name and purpose:** Claims supervisor. Choose which claims step runs next (intake, coverage, risk, critic, revision, recommendation), or finish or hand off. Sees codes and amounts only, has no tools, and never routes a claim; code checks every choice and decides the route.
- **Workflow:** Claims triage (synthetic travel insurance)
- **Owner:** Head of claims automation (`own-301`), who can stop it at once (`python -m hall_monitor.kill_switch stop`)
- **Risk tier and status:** medium, active
- **Model:** `claude-haiku-5-5`
- **Tools:** none
- **Data classes it may receive:** financial, health
- **Approved version:** manifest 0.1.0 (`2bb9ad3f4395`), prompt `claims-supervisor-v1` (`807ec7586f14`)
- **May decide alone:** Chooses the next step from a closed list; sees codes and amounts only, no tools.
- **Code forces to a person:** Can't choose auto: code routes, and auto needs every required step on the latest inputs plus the routing rule (HM-04, HM-32). Budgets, refusals and the kill switch stop the claim for a person.
- **Evidence:** [agentic-scripted](../evals/results/agentic-scripted/report.md) (scripted, 0.1.0, current)
