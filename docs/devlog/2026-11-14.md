  fixes) was built on 2026-11-11 and is described in the 2026-11-12 devlog.
- **Renumbering (`138873b`).** `main` had since used ADR 0028, HM-32 and Review 22, so the grant

- **One budget-guard interface (`e0a080a`).** `run_agent` in `hall_monitor/agents/base.py` now

  `BudgetGuard` protocol: usage read once per response, `act` defined once, one gateway path.

- Bools are refused as allotments and charges (`True` had meant $1.00). `total_cost` in

  pass-through `make_hand_off` is gone.

## Decisions

- **Renumber rather than reuse IDs.** The branch's ADR, control and review numbers had been taken
  on `main`, so the grant work took the next free ones (ADR 0031, HM-35, Review 31) instead of
  displacing merged work.
- **One budget mechanism in the tool loop.** The claims case budget and the crew grant now share
  one `BudgetGuard` interface, so they check and charge the same way rather than each its own way.
- **When in doubt, a call costs the whole grant.** A call that can't be priced mid-run exhausts
  its grant and stops the run, erring toward spending less, in line with HM-34's handling of
  unapproved budgets.
- **No crew agent without its tool gateway**, so no tool call can happen unrecorded.
- **Docs claim no more than the code** (Review 33): "every model call is charged" now notes that a
  call billed and then raised on is not.

  use `/api/public/v2/observations`.
- Does economy routing (Haiku for coverage and recommendation under $1,000) lose accuracy?
- The critic's main target, a live amount slip, didn't occur in 48 cap-test runs; is the seeded
  eval enough evidence?
- A crash after the evidence pack is logged but before the checkpoint is saved still needs a
  person (HM-05 gap). The drills now inject this case and confirm it ends with a person.
- The simulated override rate assumes adjusters agree with the answer key.
- The fairness eval varies names only, not pronouns.
- Claims history can't tell namesakes apart and nicknames still don't match.
- A non-USD or unextracted claim's payout is capped only by the senior adjuster's authority.
- A figure in both narrative and receipt still counts twice in the amount trace.
- The amount trace misses a bare `$` for another currency, a currency named only in a header, and
  `($20.00)` refunds (HM-04 gaps).
- Without `HALL_MONITOR_AUDIT_KEY` set, the HMAC key file sits beside the log.
- The stop owner ID is self-declared and the stop list is a file beside the code (HM-19 gaps).
- An in-flight call finishes after a stop; the drills saw up to 2 in-flight claims decided
  automatically in one run (HM-19 gap).
- Approvals pin prompts but not output schemas, request rendering or policy wording (HM-01 gaps).
- An answer that fails validation inside the SDK's parse call is billed but not recorded (HM-01).
- The final decision's signature covers the reason, so a guessed reason can be confirmed (HM-06).
- Request-information has no follow-up path.
- Entities encoded twice are decoded only once by the injection detector (HM-09 gap); the arena
  also found look-alike letters bypass it.
- The HR rules are a reading of 29 CFR Part 825 not yet reviewed by HR or an employment lawyer.
- CFPB complaints published after a pull has caught up are missed unless a pull restarts at least
  15 days back (HM-26 gap).
- CFPB labels outside the pinned snapshot are matched only by the routing patterns.
- The Langfuse client is not guarded by the egress policy; egress is allow-listed only on the
  complaint desk (readiness gap 8).
- CLAUDE.md still says "Synthetic data only", while the repo commits 500 real public complaints.
- Desk routing reads labels and tags, not the narrative, so must-route complaints visible only in
  the narrative go to the standard queue (HM-30, 3 of 3 in the arena).
- The draft check misses promises in plain words and links, phones, emails or promises hidden with
  entities or full-width forms (HM-30). Drafts are still never sent.
- A duplicate claim submission can still run triage twice and pay twice (HM-31).
- A kept complaint draft may quote the typed-in text from the form (HM-33).
- Is ADR 0029 (complaint form) to be moved from Proposed to Accepted now that #64 is merged?
- Who owns the spending caps and at what level? The board brief asks this; the starting caps in
  #69 were set for a demo, not tuned on live runs.
- The spend ledger covers one machine, and the evals' live runs aren't held to the caps yet
  (HM-34 gaps).
- Review 27 logged, without changing, that the supervisor loop is written three times (claims,
  desk, HR) and that one stop reason reads a misleading "unexpected UnpricedModelError".
- HM-35 gaps (#61's ADR numbering is resolved): a case can overshoot by one call per grant in its
  tree; a call the SDK bills and then raises on isn't charged; the grant book lives in memory for
  one run; a helper meant to see less personal data needs code-built input.
- New: inside a workflow with a case budget, the lead's between-round cost check doesn't see
  helpers' spending until the step returns (Review 32, plausible; left to slice 4).
- New: whether a lead treats a helper's answer as data rests on its prompt, and is measured only
