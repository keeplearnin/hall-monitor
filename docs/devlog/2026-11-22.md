## What was built

### Shared code stops importing claims (#86)

- Resolves Review 42's deferred finding: running the leave, complaint or partner example loaded
  `hall_monitor.claims`.
- Claims-only helpers moved into `hall_monitor/claims/`: `render_claim` and
  `UNTRUSTED_CLAIM_NOTICE` to `claims/agents/rendering.py`; `redact_claim` and
  `detect_instructions` to `claims/screening.py`; `TriageRecorder` to `claims/recorder.py` as a
  subclass of the shared `EventRecorder` (complaint ingest now uses `EventRecorder`); the default
  claims server, `PIPELINE_TOOLS` and `lookup_policy` to `claims/gateway.py` as `claims_gateway`.
  `render_policy_record` was removed (no callers).
- `ToolGateway` no longer defaults to the claims table: `server` and `tool_data_classes` are
  required and `pipeline_tools` defaults to none (ADR 0004 updated).
- Tests: `test_running_another_example_loads_no_claims_code` imports every module of `hr`,
  `complaints` and `partners` in a fresh interpreter and requires no `hall_monitor.claims*`
  module to load. `test_known_limit_code_serving_every_example_still_loads_claims_code` pins the
  modules that still do (the shared submit forms, their evals, `evals/agentic_compare.py`,
  `evals/stop_timing.py`).

### Tool gateway adversarial pass (#87, ADR 0035)

- `tests/test_gateway_adversarial.py` (457 lines) attacks `hall_monitor/tools/gateway.py`. Three
  holes found and fixed:
  - **Prompt injection in a tool result.** A poisoned claims-history record reached the risk agent
    and claim_018 still routed auto. The gateway now screens every string in a result (keys
    included, after redaction); the agent gets a refusal, `ToolCall.injection_signals` records the
    codes, and claims routing (`claims/routing.py`) sends the claim to a person.
  - **No scope.** `scope_policy=None` skipped the scope check. An agent with no scope can no
    longer call a tool that takes `policy_number`; the critic eval now passes the scope.
  - **Unknown arguments (`_meta`).** Now refused before the server; unknown arguments are counted,
    not named, and only the tool's own arguments are recorded.
- Attacks already refused are now guarded by tests: scope or tenant in `_meta`, `actor: pipeline`
  written by the model, look-alike tool names (case, zero-width, Cyrillic), a grant replayed into
  a new book, a tool missing from the data-class table.
- Not tested on purpose: token audience, token expiry and header/body desync, since the gateway
  takes only an in-process server. Recorded as `not_covered` (RI-26, RI-29).

### Kill-switch drill timing (#88)

- Cause of a CI flake on #87: `hall_monitor/evals/stop_timing.py` timed a call when the fake model
  was entered, after `run_agent`'s hall-pass checks, so a stop written between the two counted a
  pre-stop call as post-stop.
- The drill now wraps the registry's `require`, records each passing check, and judges a call by
  the stopped agent's last check, else the calling agent's check, else the call's start. The
  one-supervisor-turn allowance from Review 26 is removed.
- Tests in `tests/test_kill_switch.py`:
  `test_the_drill_judges_a_call_by_when_its_stop_check_ran[intake|coverage_check]` (both fail on
  the old measurement) and `test_the_drill_still_counts_a_call_whose_check_ran_after_the_stop`.
- Results in `evals/results/kill-switch-drill/` republished; coverage_check drops out of the
  drill's agent list and its model card no longer cites the drill. HM-19 updated.

### Review 46 fixes (#89, open)

- **Claims (H1, H2).** A policy number the claim's documents don't name is never looked up; a
  claim goes to a person if the documents don't name the policy number or claimant, or name more
  than one policy number. A senior adjuster must decide when the claimed total wasn't traced, or
  wasn't checked because the claim stopped or the agentic supervisor ended with steps missing.
  Changes in `claims/routing.py`, `claims/approval.py`, `claims/pipeline.py`, `claims/agentic.py`;
  tests in `tests/test_pipeline.py` and `tests/test_approval_rules.py`.
- **HR (H3, H4).** Each decision carries a keyed hash of the employee; weeks approved or extended
  on that employee's other requests come off what is left. A request that stopped before the
  rules ran needs an HR director. Changes in `hr/signoff.py`; tests in `tests/test_hr_signoff.py`.
- **Evals (H5).** A spend refusal counts as an error in the claims and desk evals.
- **Tests (H6).** New `tests/conftest.py` removes the keys and replaces dotenv's loader before any
  module imports it, so local runs never load `.env`.
- **Record (H7–H9).** Only declared tool names are copied into the record (others show as
  `<not a known tool>`) and failed calls record the exception type only; passport redaction needs
  a digit in the number; the complaint log keeps a keyed hash of the record.
- The re-review (`e7a9312`) closed three more paths: a companion's policy named beside the
  claimant's, the agentic steps-missing route counting as traced, and extensions not counted.

## Decisions

- **No implicit claims defaults in shared code.** `ToolGateway` takes its server and data-class
  table explicitly, so another example can't silently inherit the claims table (#86).
- **Tool results are untrusted input.** The gateway screens them like claim text, and a flagged
  result sends the claim to a person rather than letting the agent continue (ADR 0035).
- **Fail closed on scope and unknown arguments**, and never record model-written values the
  gateway doesn't recognise (#87, extended by H7 in #89).
- **Measure the drill at the check, not the model call.** Judging by the stop check removes the
  race and lets the drill drop its one-turn allowance, so it is stricter, not looser (#88).
- **Documents, not the intake agent, establish who and what is claimed.** Policy number, claimant
  and total must be found in the claim's documents before a claim can pay automatically or a
  junior adjuster can decide it (#89).
- **A run that tested nothing fails.** A spend refusal is an error, not a safe stop (#89).

## Open questions

- Langfuse's older read API was removed on 2026-11-16. Any eval code that reads traces back must
  use `/api/public/v2/observations`.
- Does economy routing (Haiku for coverage and recommendation under $1,000) lose accuracy?
- The critic's main target, a live amount slip, didn't occur in 48 cap-test runs; is the seeded
  eval enough evidence? (The published critic-seeded results came from unscoped runs on earlier
  agent versions, #87.)
- A crash after the evidence pack is logged but before the checkpoint is saved still needs a
  person (HM-05 gap).
- A crashed claims triage restarts from the start, pays again and gets a new case budget (HM-32).
- The claims checkpoint file is unencrypted, keeps claim text and adjusters' reasons, and is never
  deleted (HM-10).
- Narrowed by #86: code that serves every example (the shared submit forms, their evals,
  `evals/agentic_compare.py`, `evals/stop_timing.py`) still loads claims code.
- The values of a tool's own arguments are still recorded as the model wrote them (HM-03, #87).
- Token audience, expiry and header/body desync are untested; an HTTP transport would need them
  first (RI-26, RI-29).
- New (#89): the incident date is still the intake agent's; earlier leave decisions count only
  once they carry the employee hash, and there is no 12-month window; a senior adjuster's denial
  of an untraced claim has no limit.
- New (#88): the per-step re-check mutation test covers only claims, and
  `specialist_stops_after_it_answered` is gated as a sum across rows, so leave's row may go
  unexercised under load. The drill's timing has a few milliseconds of leniency (HM-19 gap).
- The simulated override rate assumes adjusters agree with the answer key.
- The fairness eval varies names only, not pronouns.
- Claims history can't tell namesakes apart and nicknames still don't match.
- A non-USD or unextracted claim's payout is capped only by the senior adjuster's authority.
- A figure in both narrative and receipt still counts twice in the amount trace.
- HM-04 amount-trace gaps: a bare `$` with no other currency named, a spaced dash (`– $20.00`),
  and currency names that are also words over-flagging.
- Duplicate documents are caught only after triage finishes; a duplicate submission can still run
  triage twice (HM-31).
- Without `HALL_MONITOR_AUDIT_KEY` set, the HMAC key file sits beside the log.
- The stop owner ID is self-declared and the stop list is a file beside the code (HM-19 gaps).
- An in-flight call finishes after a stop (HM-19 gap).
- Approvals pin prompts but not output schemas, request rendering or policy wording (HM-01 gaps).
- An answer that fails validation inside the SDK's parse call is billed but not recorded (HM-01).
- The final decision's signature covers the reason, so a guessed reason can be confirmed (HM-06).
- Signatures prove only that a key signed the bytes, and the keys are public demo keys (HM-05,
  HM-23).
- Request-information has no follow-up path.
- The injection detector and the draft check are still pattern lists (HM-09, HM-30).
- Desk routing reads labels and tags, not the narrative (3 of 18 must-route complaints missed in
  the arena).
- A changed complaint is re-routed only if a manual `--since` pull fetches it.
- The HR rules and roles are a reading of 29 CFR Part 825 not yet reviewed by HR or an employment
  lawyer.
- CFPB complaints published after a pull has caught up are missed unless a pull restarts at least
  15 days back (HM-26 gap).
- CFPB labels outside the pinned snapshot are matched only by the routing patterns.
- The Langfuse client is not guarded by the egress policy (readiness gap 8).
- CLAUDE.md still says "Synthetic data only", while the repo commits 500 real public complaints.
- A kept complaint draft may quote the typed-in text from the form (HM-33).
- Is ADR 0029 (complaint form) to be moved from Proposed to Accepted?
- Who owns the spending caps and at what level? The partners caps are estimates, not measured.
- The spend ledger covers one machine, and the evals' live runs aren't held to the caps (HM-34).
- Review 27: the supervisor loop is written three times, and one stop reason reads a misleading
  "unexpected UnpricedModelError".
- HM-35 gaps: a case can overshoot by one call per grant; a call billed and then raised on isn't
  charged; the grant book lives in memory; a workflow's case budget doesn't see helpers' spending
  between rounds.
- Whether a crew lead treats a helper's answer as data rests on its prompt.
- Partner agents' line categories and competitor and public-sector flags are trusted, not checked
  against the documents (HM-37); partner redaction doesn't remove names.
- A partner claim with activity before approval goes to review, not to the hold the rules would
  give it.
- A claim in the partner book with a bad reference still stops the book loading (HM-36).
- The live leave run has no repeat runs and no findings note (HM-18 gap).
