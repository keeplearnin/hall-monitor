## What was built

### Partner claims, slice 1: the rules (#77, HM-36, ADR 0032)

Marketing development fund (MDF) and co-op reimbursement claims at a fictional hardware maker.
No model is involved.

- `docs/partner-claims-design.md` (Proposed) and ADR 0032: the rules route each claim, a person
  decides it, nothing pays.
- `hall_monitor/partners/rules.py` sends every claim to a person by one of four routes
  (compliance review, hold, review, expedited sign-off), each finding with a fixed reason. It
  checks pre-approval and what earlier claims used of it, impossible dates and the 45-day
  window, eligible activity and evidence, third-party invoices whose lines add up, excluded
  costs, the $75 merchandise cap (from amount and units), rates more than 10% over benchmark,
  competitor content at 30%, duplicates across partners (normalised), split claims just under
  a limit, payee against the approved account, partner status and AR hold, public-sector
  hospitality, and text aimed at the AI.
- The reimbursable amount is exact decimal, capped at the claim, invoice total and
  pre-approval. The deciding role follows a dollar ladder ($5,000 / $25,000), stated as an
  assumption.
- `data/synthetic/partners/`: program terms, five fictional partners, pre-approvals, 47 claims
  with answer keys and boundary cases on both sides of every numeric rule.
- `python -m hall_monitor.partners` scores the rules against the keys.

### Partner claims, slice 2: the agents (#78, HM-37, ADR 0033)

- `partner_claim_intake` (Haiku, no tools, financial data only) extracts invoice number,
  issuer, lines with units, total and activity dates from redacted, fenced documents.
- `check_extraction` verifies every extracted value against the document text (whole-token
  invoice number, issuer as a whole part of a line, total on the total line, a line's amount
  and units on one line); an unsupported value adds `extraction_unsupported`.
- `partner_proof_reviewer` (Sonnet, no tools) returns concerns from a closed list of five plus
  a confidence, no free text. A concern only adds review; a clean review never clears a rule
  finding.
- Portal fields (partner, pre-approval, payee, amount claimed) never come from an agent.
- A failed read holds the whole claim with nothing reimbursable and keeps every rule finding
  that needs no invoice (`portal_reasons` in `rules.py`).
- Synthetic documents for 11 claims, reviewer answer keys, manifests (owner `own-601`),
  approvals and model cards.

### Review 38 fixes (`review-fixes-nov15`, local)

- **Partner claims (`118ee79`, findings 1–6).** Unit counts must be written as units, and
  units × unit price must make the line amount (`units_not_in_invoice`); activity start and
  end must be the pair written together; the issuer must come before any Bill to / Client
  label; `prepare()` re-checks every agent that answered (`agent_stopped`); an unknown partner
  or pre-approval goes to a person instead of crashing; a `partners` workflow budget in
  `registry/budgets.yaml` (re-approved) with `spend_refused` and `budget_exhausted`. Tests in
  `tests/test_partner_agents.py`.
- **Claims.** Same documents under a new claim ID go to an adjuster via a keyed
  `documents_hmac` in each triage result (`be02071`, `duplicate_documents`). Accounting
  negatives (`($200.00)`, `$150.00 CR`) are refunds (`e196afb`). A currency named anywhere in
  the documents (a CAD header, `C$`, `€`) means only USD or US$ figures trace (`46bc37a`,
  `hall_monitor/amounts.py`). `registry/adjusters.yaml` refuses duplicate IDs and non-finite
  or non-positive limits and is pinned by digest in `registry/approvals.yaml` (`0731f86`). A
  stop during a revision round halts it before the next agent (`423724a`,
  `hall_monitor/agentic.py`).
- **Complaint desk.** A routed complaint whose stored version changed gets an additive
  `complaint_rerouted` entry to specialist review (`record_changed`), never lowering the first
  route (`dc85786`). The draft check is now strict, read raw and decoded: digit runs, domains
  and spelled-out links, handles, addresses, extensions, "ask for", promises and deadlines
  (`15aac06`, `dc85786`). The injection detector knows the desk's own vocabulary
  (`steers_the_triage`), decodes entities until stable and maps Cyrillic and Greek look-alikes
  (`hall_monitor/gateway/injection.py`). A triage agent stopped after answering has its answer
  and draft discarded.
- **HR.** Non-ASCII form tokens give a 403, not a 500, and `--max-requests` is capped at 1–50
  (`9e56d1e`, `hall_monitor/hr/submit.py`). Qualifying-exigency and military-caregiver leave
  without a verified covered status route to HR review (`military_status_unverified`); answer
  keys `lr_020` and `lr_026` changed and are logged (`26f6a07`).
- **Audit and gate.** `verify` compares each trigger's stored SQL with the expected SQL and
  checks `leave_decision` entries against their signed records, listing missing ones as
  `decision_records_erased` (`d8d3d05`). The eval gate fails NaN, infinities, booleans and
  non-numbers (`1f23723`, `hall_monitor/evals/gate.py`).

## Decisions

- **Partner claims: rules route, a person decides, nothing pays** (ADR 0032). No code in the
  package can pay, approve or change a payee.
- **Partner agents read and flag; code checks** (ADR 0033). Extracted values are verified
  against document text, the reviewer has a closed list and no free text, and portal fields
  never come from an agent, so an agent can add review but never clear a finding.
- **The dollar ladder is labelled an assumption**, since no vendor publishes thresholds.
- **Leave roles follow the org chart a branch company actually has** (ADR 0034), so the role
  check doesn't enforce roles nobody holds.
- **Military leave the records can't verify goes to a person**, who may attest, rather than
  being approved on a status the records don't carry.
- **Re-routes are additive.** A changed complaint gets a new log entry and never lowers the
  first route, keeping the log append-only.
- **The adjusters file is approved by digest**, like the budgets: decisions are rejected while
  it isn't the approved version, because a NaN limit would let any payout pass.
- **A live result on approved agents is gated.** The first one (leave path) is a required check,
  rather than results that only warn when stale.

- A crash after the evidence pack is logged but before the checkpoint is saved still needs a
  person (HM-05 gap).

- The fairness eval varies names only, not pronouns.
- Claims history can't tell namesakes apart and nicknames still don't match.
- A non-USD or unextracted claim's payout is capped only by the senior adjuster's authority.
- A figure in both narrative and receipt still counts twice in the amount trace.

  are handled; still open are a bare `$` with no other currency named, a spaced dash
  (`– $20.00`), and currency names that are also words over-flagging.
- Duplicate documents are caught only after triage finishes (two at once can miss), on the same
  extracted policy, attachments only, and in the workflow only (Review 38, finding 7). A
  duplicate submission can still run triage twice (HM-31).
- Without `HALL_MONITOR_AUDIT_KEY` set, the HMAC key file sits beside the log.

- An in-flight call finishes after a stop (HM-19 gap).
- Approvals pin prompts but not output schemas, request rendering or policy wording (HM-01 gaps).
- An answer that fails validation inside the SDK's parse call is billed but not recorded (HM-01).
- The final decision's signature covers the reason, so a guessed reason can be confirmed (HM-06).
- Signatures prove only that a key signed the bytes, and the keys are public demo keys (HM-05,
  HM-23; third-party review).
- Request-information has no follow-up path.
- The injection detector and the draft check are wider on the branch (entities decoded until
  stable, look-alikes mapped, strict draft check), but both are still pattern lists (HM-09,
  HM-30).

  the narrative go to the standard queue (3 of 18 in the arena).
- A changed complaint is re-routed only if a manual `--since` pull fetches it; ingest's cursor
  only moves forward (finding 12 gap).
- The HR rules and roles are a reading of 29 CFR Part 825 not yet reviewed by HR or an
  employment lawyer.
- CFPB complaints published after a pull has caught up are missed unless a pull restarts at
  least 15 days back (HM-26 gap).
- CFPB labels outside the pinned snapshot are matched only by the routing patterns.
- The Langfuse client is not guarded by the egress policy (readiness gap 8).
- CLAUDE.md still says "Synthetic data only", while the repo commits 500 real public complaints.
- A kept complaint draft may quote the typed-in text from the form (HM-33).
- Is ADR 0029 (complaint form) to be moved from Proposed to Accepted?
- Who owns the spending caps and at what level? The partners caps (1.00 a day, 10.00 a month)
  are estimates, not measured.
- The spend ledger covers one machine, and the evals' live runs aren't held to the caps yet
  (HM-34 gaps).
- Review 27: the supervisor loop is written three times (claims, desk, HR), and one stop reason
  reads a misleading "unexpected UnpricedModelError".
- HM-35 gaps: a case can overshoot by one call per grant; a call billed and then raised on isn't

  code-built input. A workflow's case budget doesn't see helpers' spending between rounds.
- Whether a crew lead treats a helper's answer as data rests on its prompt.
- New: partner agents' line categories and competitor and public-sector flags are trusted, not

- New: partner redaction is pattern-based and doesn't remove names (HM-37).
